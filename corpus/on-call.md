# On-call

## Rotation

The primary on-call rotation is one week long and hands over every Monday at 10:00 UTC. Each rotation has a primary and a secondary engineer. If the primary does not acknowledge a page within 5 minutes, the page escalates to the secondary, and after a further 10 minutes to the engineering manager.

## Compensation and load

On-call engineers receive a flat stipend per rotation week plus time off in lieu for any page handled between 22:00 and 07:00 local time. A rotation that receives more than 10 pages in a week triggers an alert-quality review before the next handover.

## Handover

The outgoing primary writes a handover note in the on-call log that lists open incidents, noisy alerts, and any risky deploys scheduled for the coming week.
